🚀 Overview

ADEIP (AI-Assisted Digital Evidence Intelligence Platform) is a multi-agent investigation platform designed to help authorized investigators analyze, organize, correlate, and understand digital evidence from multiple sources.

Modern investigations can involve large volumes of fragmented information such as:

💻 System and application logs
🌐 Network and firewall logs
📹 CCTV footage
🔐 Access-control records
💾 USB and device activity
📧 Emails and documents
📂 Digital forensic artifacts

ADEIP brings these sources into a unified investigation workspace and uses specialized AI agents to extract events, correlate evidence, reconstruct timelines, visualize relationships, and generate explainable investigative leads.

⚠️ ADEIP assists human investigators. It does not determine guilt, replace investigators, or make autonomous legal decisions.

🎯 Problem Statement

Digital investigations often involve evidence distributed across multiple systems and formats.

Investigators may need to manually:

Analyze thousands of log entries
Compare timestamps from different sources
Review CCTV footage
Identify relationships between people, devices, and accounts
Correlate network activity
Reconstruct a sequence of events
Identify missing or useful evidence

This process can be time-consuming and complex.

ADEIP aims to help transform fragmented evidence into structured and explainable investigative intelligence.
